# 📊 Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview
An interactive Retail Sales Analytics Dashboard developed using Microsoft Power BI and DAX to analyze sales performance, profitability, and customer demographics.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Sales Analysis
- Total Sales and YoY Sales Growth
- Sales by Category and Segment
- Top 5 Selling Products
- Monthly Sales Comparison
- Country-wise Sales Performance

### 2. Profit Analysis
- Total Profit and YoY Profit Growth
- Profit by Category and Segment
- Top 5 Profitable Products
- Monthly Profit Comparison

### 3. Demographic Analysis
- Top 10 Active Buyers
- Top 10 Valuable Customers
- Top 6 Valuable States
- Profit by Order Priority
- Sales and Profit by Region

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX Measures
- Data Visualization
- Business Intelligence

## Key Learnings
- Creating DAX measures and KPIs
- Developing interactive dashboards
- Analyzing sales and profit trends
- Generating meaningful business insights
